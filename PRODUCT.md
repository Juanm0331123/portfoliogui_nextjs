# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, technical leads, potential clients, and collaborators who need to quickly understand Juan Miguel Leon Gomez's professional profile, technical focus, project quality, and reliability as a programmer and informatics engineer.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Juan Miguel's services, projects, experience, and contact path through a polished personal brand site. Success means visitors can understand his technical value, trust his professional taste, inspect real work, and reach out without friction.

## Brand Personality

Professional, precise, and premium. The interface should feel technical and confident without becoming cold, crowded, or generic.

## Anti-references

Avoid layouts where the portrait dominates the content, text blocks feel visually unbalanced, or the section looks like a generic developer template. Avoid changing the established dark cosmic background, purple/cyan palette, and animated portfolio identity.

## Design Principles

- Keep the personal brand visually strong, but let content and readability lead.
- Balance portrait, copy, and actions so no single element overwhelms the section.
- Preserve the existing color identity while improving hierarchy and spacing.
- Make every section feel production-ready on mobile and desktop.
- Prefer clear technical confidence over inflated marketing language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for text contrast, responsive readability, keyboard access, and reduced-motion accommodations where animation is used.
